# GitHub research patterns used

The skill combines patterns observed in public repositories searched on 2026-08-14. These are inspiration and implementation evidence, not dependencies.

- [louisedesadeleer/b-roll-finder](https://github.com/louisedesadeleer/b-roll-finder): interpret the whole transcript, route beats to authoritative sources, keep a taste profile, place cutaways after the spoken word, preserve a manifest, and verify with contact sheets.
- [video-production-buddy/video-production-buddy](https://github.com/video-production-buddy/video-production-buddy): use retrieval-first footage adapters and an asset engine with license provenance.
- [Agents365-ai/video-podcast-maker](https://github.com/Agents365-ai/video-podcast-maker): maintain a per-video asset manifest and separate user files, stock, AI stills, AI video, and overlays; gate paid generation.
- [Jackywxsz/Jacky-motion](https://github.com/Jackywxsz/Jacky-motion): treat SRT as the only master clock, cover every interval, and make B-roll a formal beat rather than an empty placeholder.
- [ScreenKite/awesome-ai-video-editing](https://github.com/ScreenKite/awesome-ai-video-editing): transcribe and proofread proper nouns before rendering, confirm density before scaffolding, keep entry/hold timelines readable, and render serially to avoid browser contention.
- [pyang5166/gbro-collage-broll](https://github.com/pyang5166/gbro-collage-broll): use explicit approval gates between visual metaphor, still generation, and animation for higher-cost creative routes.

The `dy_1024` adaptation intentionally adds stricter evidence/rights rules, the existing Gate 3 fingerprint contract, the AI Sentinel no-raster-evidence policy, and the one-owner-per-scene motion contract.
