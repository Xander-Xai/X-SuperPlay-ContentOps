# Duration router

Choose a duration tier from content complexity, evidence density, viewer job
and platform constraints. Supported examples are 30s, 60s, 90s and 180s;
the account profile remains authoritative when it defines other tiers.

The router records the reason for the choice and rejects padding, repeated
conclusions and forced compression that would remove necessary proof. The
selected target and range must be numeric and internally consistent; the
component receipt must verify the declared range rather than only checking that
the fields exist.
