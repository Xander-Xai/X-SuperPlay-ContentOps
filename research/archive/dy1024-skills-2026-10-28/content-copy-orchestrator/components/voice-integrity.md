# Voice and integrity pass

Input: canonical script, claims map, account voice profile and risk policy.

Output: a humanized canonical script plus a QC receipt. Preserve claims,
numbers, technical terms, limitations and attribution. Remove throat-clearing,
generic AI phrasing, fake first-hand experience and unsupported certainty.

The pass may rewrite wording, but it may not change the editorial angle or
invent evidence.
