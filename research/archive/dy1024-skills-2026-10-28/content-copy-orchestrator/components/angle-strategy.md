# Angle strategy

Input: Brief, audience, viewer problem, evidence map and account voice.

Output: one selected audience, one viewer promise, one registered editorial
angle, one counter-expectation or tension, one payoff, and one CTA. Record the
selected angle ID in `copy-strategy.json` and `strategy_angle_id` in the
canonical script.

Generate 3–8 registered candidates, score them by audience relevance, evidence
strength, visual proof and actionability, then commit to one. The strategy
receipt must say `angle_library=applied` and include the selected ID. Do not
produce multiple competing scripts.
