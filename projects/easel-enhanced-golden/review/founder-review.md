## Rubric

The same questions, in the same order, for every arm.

1. **voice_naturalness** — Does the voice sound like a person speaking, and is it easy to follow?
2. **visual_consistency** — Does the video look like one piece, or do the visual sources clash?
3. **pacing** — Do the cuts and durations hold attention without dragging or rushing?
4. **generated_image_usefulness** — Where present, does the generated image add understanding, or is it decoration?
5. **h3_usefulness** — Where present, does the H3 insert earn its place, or would the video be tighter without it?
6. **artifact_severity** — Any deformation, garbled text, bad frame or unwanted audio?
7. **overall_publishability** — Would you publish this as-is?
