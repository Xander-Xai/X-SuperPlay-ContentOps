# Writing recipes and worked original drafts

Use these as **mechanisms**, not as boilerplate text. Choose the angle from real material, then write fresh sentences. The examples below are *original demonstrations*, not collected X posts and **have no observed engagement metrics**.

## Core structure: Tension, Insight, Invitation

A useful reach-oriented post often fits:

1. **Hook:** an explicit contradiction, scene, surprising observation, or concrete number.
2. **Payload:** a real example, mechanism, insight, or short laugh — not an empty headline.
3. **Invitation (optional):** a question that readers can answer from experience, or a one-line finish worth quoting.

No more than one primary idea and one principal question per short post. Never fabricate authority or imply the reader must comment/follow to obtain a reward.

**Disallowed variant:** "Reply X, I guarantee Y followers". The source corpus contains many instances; treat them as negative examples of transactional follow-bait, not as an optimization formula.

## Four complete demonstrations (each is a fictional editorial exercise)

### A. Chinese X growth / algorithm anxiety

**Input thesis:** A creator sees a high-view post followed by weaker reach. No platform analytics supplied.

Variant A (contrast):

> 一条帖子爆了，最容易让人误会的不是算法，而是自己的预期。
>
> 昨天十万人围观，今天几百人互动，就以为被限流了。
>
> 可爆款带来的是一次注意力，不是下一条的保底流量。
>
> 你们有没有经历过这种落差？

Variant B (punchline):

> 玩X最像坐过山车的瞬间：
>
> 昨天觉得自己马上要做大V，
>
> 今天开始怀疑是不是发帖按钮坏了。
>
> 但流量波动，不等于有人偷偷关了开关。

Why: both use P01 but test **empathetic question vs humorous finish**; neither pretends to know X ranking internals.

### B. Technical creator / practical value

**Input thesis:** A developer made an Agent prototype but has no production reliability metrics yet.

Variant A (engineering reversal):

> 让17个Agent同时说话，已经不是什么难题。
>
> 真正难的是：第8个工具超时了，前面7步还能不能恢复？
>
> Demo展示能力，失败恢复才展示工程。
>
> 你们做Agent，最常卡在哪一环？

Variant B (one-line contrast):

> AI Agent最贵的部分，可能不是模型调用费。
>
> 而是每一次失败之后，没人知道它究竟做过什么。
>
> 我更愿意看一张可追踪的执行日志，而不是再看一张17个Bot的组织架构图。

Why: P10/P07. Avoids false claims such as "I deployed 17 agents with 99% success".

### C. Lifestyle / food without a fabricated personal account

**Input thesis:** A user shares a real photo of winter wonton soup and has permission to use it.

Variant A (sensory):

> 冬天最舒服的时刻，可能不是盖上厚被子。
>
> 是手里捧着一碗刚出锅的馄饨，先喝一口汤。
>
> 你们那边冬天最治愈的一口是什么？

Variant B (humor):

> 冬天有一种幸福很便宜：
>
> 小馄饨加点紫菜虾皮，连汤喝干净。
>
> 心情好不好先放一边，胃至少先被哄好了。

Why: P07/P08. The model should not invent a restaurant, its location, pricing or the taste if no one tasted it.

### D. English / authentic low-key reflection

**Input thesis:** User wants a quotable English post about attention and boundaries.

Variant A:

> Your peace gets louder when you stop explaining yourself to people who already decided not to listen.

Variant B:

> Not every notification deserves your energy. Some of the best progress happens when your phone stays quiet.

Why: P15; concise, natural English, no pseudo-guarantees and no mandatory "Who's in?".

## Additional seed angles — transform, don't repost

| Input observation or sample category | New opening angle | Substance to include |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts brag about 1,000 comments | "把互动当计件工作..." | discuss marginal value, fatigue, quality |
| "手搓" vs AI replies | "真人的优势不是打字更快..." | specificity, firsthand observation, reply depth |
| Commenters don't reciprocate | "有来有往不是KPI..." | community reciprocity not entitlement |
| Inexpensive creator-income dream | "‘月入1000美元’少说了三个词..." | variability, costs, policy and effort |
| Algorithm shadowban rumor | "流量下降不等于惩罚..." | age-window and content mix |
| Long engineering study vs AI | "会写代码的门槛低了..." | diagnosis, validation, ownership |
| A book and an old song | "旋律记得比人更牢..." | one concrete memory image |
| Cat claims the stairs | "有些台阶先归猫管..." | light anthropomorphism |
| Food made at home | "一锅蒸菜最大的优点..." | actual preparation/time only if user has it |
| A long viral thread on celebrity | "消息越爆炸，越该先找公告..." | official status and unknowns |
| X -> other platform reposts | "信息差不是原创能力..." | licensing/attribution and new insight |
| Safety/privacy hot topic | "事实没查清，转发可能先伤人..." | privacy-preserving viewpoint |
| New AI model launches | "参数表越长，我越想看..." | real benchmarks, workload and limits |
| Skilled operator vs AI tool | "用AI是一个人，组织AI是一套流程..." | retries/observability and accountability |
| Viral creator's income screenshot | "截图展示收入，不展示波动..." | verify net income and time period |
| English "say a keyword" gimmick | "Engagement isn't a keyword..." | genuine community value, not a scam |

## Built-in prompt: when asked to "make 5 X posts"

Use this internally:

> Write 5 distinct **original X main posts** for [confirmed account, language, audience], drawing only on the mechanisms in corpus-taxonomy.md and the current topic evidence. At least 3 different hook forms; no copied user-provided post; no manufactured personal anecdotes, metrics or named-person rumors. Balance 2 insight/utility, 1 emotional/identity, 1 relatable humor, 1 open debate if the audience supports it. Each should stand alone, have one specific central idea, and not sound AI-templated. Give only final copy unless the user asks for reasoning. If an account is unconfirmed or incompatible, mark publication review as pending. For a test, add a labeled hypothesis rather than claiming viral potential as measured fact.

**Red-team rewrite check:** If any candidate begins "哈哈哈/卧槽/不懂就问/说个暴论" and all candidates share that phrase, rewrite at least most of them with a specific claim or scene.
